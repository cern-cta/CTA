# Contributing

Contributions to CTA are welcome. Discuss your proposed work on the [community forum](https://cta-community.web.cern.ch/) or email [cta-support@cern.ch](mailto:cta-support@cern.ch) before starting. Agree on the scope first, especially for changes to architecture, interfaces, or behaviour that affect other components.

## Choose your contribution route

**CERN GitLab is the primary contribution route.** CTA development, issue tracking, CI, review, and merging take place there. Contributors with a CERN computing account and CTA project permissions should follow [Contributing through CERN GitLab](gitlab.md).

**GitHub is available for tasks agreed with the CTA team in advance**, for contributors without CERN developer access. Before starting work or opening a pull request, agree on the task’s scope and confirm that a maintainer is available to handle the GitLab handoff. Then follow [Contributing through GitHub](github.md).

The GitHub handoff requires manual review and synchronization by a maintainer. Daily updates and fixed review turnaround times are not guaranteed.

## Keep contributions focused

Aim for one clear outcome per contribution. Include relevant tests and documentation, and split unrelated work into separate changes. For an introduction to the local edit/build/test cycle, see [Your First Change](../getting-started/first-change.md).

Use [Prerequisites & Access](../getting-started/prerequisites.md) to prepare your checkout, [Changelog Entries](changelog.md) for changelog requirements, and [Documentation Changes](documentation.md) when editing the documentation. Release and infrastructure procedures belong in [For Maintainers](maintainers/index.md).
