# Contributing to CTA

Contributions to CTA are welcome. Before starting, discuss your proposed work on the [community forum](https://cta-community.web.cern.ch/) or email [cta-support@cern.ch](mailto:cta-support@cern.ch) to agree on the scope and contribution route.

## Contribution routes

- **CERN GitLab is the primary route.** Development, CI, review, and merging take place there. Direct developer access requires a CERN computing account and CTA project permissions. See [Prerequisites & Access](docs/content/dev/getting-started/prerequisites.md) and [Contributing through CERN GitLab](docs/content/dev/contributing/gitlab.md).
- **GitHub is available for tasks agreed with the team in advance**, for contributors without CERN developer access. Before starting work or opening a pull request, confirm that a maintainer is available to handle the GitLab handoff. Review and synchronization require manual work and happen as maintainer time permits. See [Contributing through GitHub](docs/content/dev/contributing/github.md).

## Get started

Read the [Contributing overview](docs/content/dev/contributing/index.md) for contribution guidelines, or follow [Your First Change](docs/content/dev/getting-started/first-change.md) for the path from a checkout to review. Detailed workflow, testing, and maintenance procedures are maintained in the documentation.
